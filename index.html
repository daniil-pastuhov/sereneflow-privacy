Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

SereneFlow is a routine timer that works entirely on your device. There is no account, no developer server, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. The app does not collect any personal data, and it has no internet access at all.

What the app stores

Your routines, their steps and your settings (theme, sounds, reminders) are saved in files inside the app on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Voice guidance

Spoken step announcements use your device's built-in text-to-speech (Apple's speech synthesis on iPhone and iPad, the system text-to-speech engine on Android). The step names are spoken on your device.

Notifications and background

If you let SereneFlow follow your routine with the screen off, it shows the current step and time left in a notification (Android) or a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island (iPhone). These are created on your device and are not sent anywhere. You can turn this off in Settings at any time.

Device backups

Your phone's own backup (Google or iCloud) may include the app's data. That is controlled by your device settings, not by us.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and it does not collect data from anyone.

Your control

You can edit or delete any routine inside the app. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
