Where are my routines?

Your routines are stored only on your device. There is no account and no server, so we cannot see or recover them.

I don't hear the voice or the bowl

  • Check the routine's sound setting in the routine editor, and that the speaker button on the timer screen isn't muted.
  • Turn up the media volume. On iPhone, SereneFlow plays even with the ring/silent switch set to silent.
  • Voice guidance uses your device's text-to-speech. On Android, make sure a text-to-speech engine with an English voice is installed (Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech).

The timer stops when the screen turns off

Turn on Follow routine with screen off in Settings. On Android, also allow notifications for SereneFlow. The timer always keeps the right time, but with this off it only guides you while the app is open.

Deleting your data

Delete routines in the app, or uninstall the app.
